About Me

Selected Projects & Contributions

Flood Mitigation - Course-Based Project

A coursework project involving the development of case studies on nature-based solutions for mitigating flood risk in different locations around the world.

Role: Student

Contribution: I simulated flood mitigation projects for Lagos State, Nigeria, and Norwich, United Kingdom. These projects enhanced my understanding of flood risks to physical infrastructure, including buildings and roads, and the economic benefits of flood mitigation. It also highlighted the importance of nature-based solutions, such as agroforestry, conservation agriculture, and forest conservation, in improving soil infiltration and supporting environmental health.
